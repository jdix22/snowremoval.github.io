What happens on a storm night

A typical overnight storm on a Season pass. You don't have to do anything.

  1. The day before

    We watch the forecast

    Dispatch assigns routes and crews. You get a text if snow is expected overnight.

  2. When snow starts

    Trucks wait at the yard

    Plows are fueled, salt is loaded, and crews stand by until 2 inches is down.

  3. At 2 inches

    First pass

    Routes run in order. In storms over 12 inches we return every 4 inches so it never gets too heavy.

  4. Before 7 a.m.

    Final pass and salt

    Walkways cleared, salt down, end-of-drive ridge gone. Your photo arrives by text.

What if a car is parked in the driveway?

We clear around it and come back once that day at no charge. Text us the night before and we'll plan around your schedule.

Will the plow damage my lawn or driveway?

We mark your driveway edges with reflective stakes in November, before the first storm. If we damage turf or pavement, we repair it in spring at our cost.

What happens when less than 2 inches falls?

Season pass customers get walkways and steps salted. If you'd like the driveway cleared too, text us and we'll add it for $25.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry $2 million in general liability and commercial auto coverage. Commercial customers get a certificate of insurance on request.

What if it's a light winter?

The Season pass price stays the same either way. If you'd rather pay only when it snows, choose Per storm.
